- Gain-encoded filenames contain unsuffixed `P/I/D` alongside `Po/Io/Do` or `Pd/Id/Dd`. These suggest inner-controller versus outer/digital-controller settings, but are filename annotations rather than verified MTS configuration exports.
- The latest dated name is `20260626_1838_sp1_dmg_P30_I30_Po_015_IO_20_DO_00015.mat`: it notes P30/I30; it does not specify an unsuffixed D.
- June 17 names explicitly note `D0001`; preserve that literal encoding rather than assuming its decimal interpretation. `Pvar` indicates varying P; its time history is not established from the name.
- The gain-named MAT files inspected do not have explicit top-level inner/MTS PID parameter fields.
- The August 26 baseline stores `result.pid_values` = P 0.12, I 12, D 0.0012, N 512. These are the Simulink digital controller gains; they do not establish the MTS controller settings.
- `docs/work_done/Updates_06_18.pptx`, slide 68, discusses increasing inner P then finding I, and notes a low D gain of 0.001 improved stability; the text does not unambiguously assign that D to one loop. Slides 74-75 explicitly describe varying inner P gain online. Slide 68 has an MTS tuning photo, but its gain-value fields are obscured; visible small numbers appear to be adjustment increments and should not be read as actual gains.
